Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, policies, and booking process

How far in advance should I book my appointment?

We recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance for regular services and 3-4 weeks for special events like bridal services or color transformations.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Just bring yourself! We'll provide everything you need. For hair services, it's helpful to bring photos of styles you like.

Do you offer packages for special events?

Yes! We offer bridal packages, special event packages, and group bookings. Contact us for custom pricing and availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 24-hour notice for cancellations. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee. Please call us if you need to reschedule.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Absolutely! Gift certificates are available for any dollar amount or specific services. They never expire and make perfect gifts.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards, and digital payments including Apple Pay and Google Pay.
